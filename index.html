Central Government Health Scheme (CGHS)

Looking for a CGHS Panel Dental Clinic in Delhi?

Contact White Oak Dental Clinic to confirm current CGHS empanelment status, applicable dental treatments, patient eligibility, dispensary referral requirements, and documentation needed prior to your visit.

1

Check Referral Requirements

Confirm whether a formal referral permission letter is required from your designated CGHS Wellness Centre / CMO for your specific dental procedure.

2

Carry Valid Plastic Card & ID

Bring your original CGHS plastic beneficiary card with beneficiary ID along with a valid Government photo ID proof (Aadhaar or departmental ID).

3

Call Desk Before Traveling

Telephone our helpdesk at +91 99991 08937 to confirm current CGHS active empanelment, designated doctor slot, and treatment coverage.

Notice for Beneficiaries: CGHS empanelment terms, cashless eligibility, and admissible dental procedure tariffs are governed strictly by the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare guidelines.
